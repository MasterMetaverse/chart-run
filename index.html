      How the pig plays

      Run the chart.

      1. Run and jump

        ◀ ▶ or ← → to run. JUMP or Space to jump; hold it to jump higher. Green candles go up, red candles go down.

      2. Grab the sats

        Blue ₿ coins are the holders' 81%. Every coin is 1,000 sats in your stack.

      3. Bump TRADE blocks

        Hit them from below. Every trade pays tax: coins pop out, sometimes a power-up, and Argus' cut flies off.

      4. Stomp the scammers

        Jump on Scam DM bots and bears. Chain stomps without landing for ×2, ×4, ×8. Touching them from the side costs a heart.

      5. Watch your step

        Rug pits swallow a heart. A red arrow means a dump candle is about to fall.

      6. Power-ups

        Pink drop: liquidity shield, blocks one hit. Claim: pulls nearby sats in. Diamond: Diamond Hands, run through anything. Heart: +1 heart.

      7. Reach the ATH flag

        The higher you grab it, the bigger the bonus, plus time left and a level bonus. Each level is a new market phase and harder than the last.

      8. Compete

        Daily: same levels for everyone today. Degen: 1 heart, faster enemies, double sats. Share a run to send friends a link with your score to beat.
